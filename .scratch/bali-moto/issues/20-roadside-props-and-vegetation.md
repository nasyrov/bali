# Grilling: roadside props and vegetation

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 14, 17
Map: ../map.md

## Question

What fills the world beside the roads, and how is it placed?

Decide the prop set (coconut palms, banana and other trees, rice paddy clumps and terrace walls, warungs and roadside stalls, penjor poles, shrines, signs, walls and gates, parked scooters), how each is generated in the golden-hour low-poly style, the placement rules per land use (paddy on farmland, palms along the coast and roads, dense walls and stalls in built-up areas), density per road class and per region, and how placement is made deterministic per chunk so the same road always looks the same.

## Context from the terrain ticket

Terrace steps on sloped farmland are synthesised in the terrain itself with vertical walls, and ground colour per land-cover class comes from vertex colours. This ticket only dresses them: wall texture props, paddy clumps, palms along terrace edges. Rivers already have channels and water ribbons.

## Context from the traffic ticket

Parked scooters and cars are props placed by this ticket's pipeline, with colliders: scooters along gangs and outside warungs, cars on residential streets, density by land use.

## Context from the buildings ticket

Compound walls, shopfront awnings and temple kits are generated by the buildings pipeline, not this one. This ticket covers free-standing props: palms and trees, paddy clumps, terrace-wall dressing, warungs and stalls where no footprint exists, penjor poles, shrines, signs, parked vehicles, and airport planes.

## Answer

**Kit** (v1), all low-poly in the golden-hour palette:
- Vegetation: coconut palm in three heights, banana plant, frangipani, banyan, generic broadleaf tree, bamboo clump, rice clump, mangrove.
- Roadside culture: penjor pole, small shrine with offerings, warung stall for lots without a footprint, motorbike-rental sign, roadside banner, temple umbrella.
- Infrastructure: street lamp, power pole with sagging wires, road sign, bollards, low retaining wall dressing for terraces, drainage channel edge.
- Beach and coast: sun lounger, surfboard rack, beach umbrella, jukung fishing boat, sea wall.
- Parked vehicles from the traffic kit (scooters, cars) and airport planes from the landmarks decision.

**Sourcing**: generic vegetation, vehicles and street furniture from CC0 low-poly packs (Kenney, Quaternius or equivalent), recoloured to the palette; Bali-specific pieces (penjor, shrine, candi bentar, warung, jukung, the temple kit, the player scooter) hand-modelled in Blender. Everything exported as glTF into the asset kit, with a licence note per file feeding the attribution screen.

**Placement**: rule-based, deterministic scatter in the pipeline. Each land-cover class has a density table per prop (paddy clumps and terrace-wall dressing on farmland, forest trees on wood, mangrove on wetland, beach set on beach, palms on the coastal strip). Road edges have their own rules by class: palms and power poles along main roads, penjor and shrines in gangs, lamps on tertiary and above at the day/night ticket's spacing, warung stalls on commercial edges without footprints, parked scooters along gangs and outside warungs, parked cars on residential streets. Placement hashes chunk id and cell so every build is identical; it avoids road ribbons, building footprints, water, terrace walls and landmark footprints, and honours OSM `natural=tree` points where mapped.

**Density**: full density within about 80 m of roads and everywhere in tier 0; forest and paddy interiors further from roads get about a third of the density with larger merged instances.

**Rendering**: tier 0 chunks batch all props into one `BatchedMesh` draw per material, switching to a simplified mesh beyond 60 m; tier 1 keeps only large vegetation as crossed billboards; tier 2 has no props. Props near the bike carry simple colliders: trunks, walls, lamps, poles, parked vehicles, boats.

**Motion**: a vertex-shader wind offset by height and time on palm fronds, banana leaves and banners, amplitude driven by the live wind speed from the weather ticket. Everything else is static.
